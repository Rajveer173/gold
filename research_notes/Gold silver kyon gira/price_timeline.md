## Q3. When did the falls start, how big were they (peak-to-trough % and absolute), over how many days? Which were the largest single-day falls?

### Takeaway
There were **several separate falls**, not one:
1. **21 Oct 2025:** gold -6.3%, the biggest one-day drop since 2013.
2. **29 Dec 2025:** a profit-taking hit to the year-end records.
3. **The 30 Jan–2 Feb 2026 crash:** gold -9.5% in one day (biggest since 1983) plus a further -6.1%; silver -28% to -31% in one day (worst since March 1980).
4. **March 2026:** gold -12% in the month, the worst month since June 2013, during the Iran war.
5. **A slow grind to a July 2026 low** of ~$4,039 (-28% from the peak).
6. **Fed-hike pressure in Sep–Oct 2026.**
7. **The November 2026 leg:** gold fell from ~$4,121 (5 Nov) to **$3,513.54 (20 Nov)**, its lowest since Sept 2025, with the worst week since March.

**From peak to trough (to 20 Nov 2026):** gold **-37%** (about -$2,076/oz) and silver **-66%** (about -$80.5/oz) (calc.). On MCX, gold fell about **-32.5%** (about -₹58,700/10g) and silver about **-64%** (about -₹2.6 lakh/kg) (calc.). The whole fall took about **10 months**: 29 Jan to 20 Nov 2026 is roughly 200 trading days.

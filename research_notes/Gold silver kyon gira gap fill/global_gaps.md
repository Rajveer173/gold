## Q2. March 2026: worst month since June 2013 or since October 2008?

### Takeaway
**BEST-SUPPORTED:** Both headlines are "right" on different bases and at different times. The **WGC's official figure is -12% to $4,608/oz, the weakest month since June 2013**. WGC uses the LBMA Gold Price PM, month-end to month-end. CNBC's "worst since 2008" (31 Mar) and Trading Economics' "more than 13%, steepest since October 2008" were spot-based readings taken **before the month closed**, when spot was near $4,570. Use the WGC number on screen.

### Cited Findings
- WGC: "Gold fell 12% in March to US$4,608/oz, its weakest month since June 2013." The fall was driven mostly by momentum (ETF outflows, COMEX net-long unwind). Gold fell in all major currencies but was still up for the year. — [WGC Gold Market Commentary, March 2026 ("Anatomy of a fall")](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-market-commentary-march-2026); [ANI via LA Herald](https://www.losangelesherald.com/news/278974851/gold-prices-record-worst-monthly-drop-since-2013-with-12-fall-in-march-world-gold-council) (cross-checked)
- Gold ETFs lost US$12bn (84t) in March, almost all from North America. — [WGC March commentary](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-market-commentary-march-2026) (via search summary)
- Trading Economics (31 Mar): "Gold Tops $4,570 but Heads for Worst Monthly Drop Since 2008", with a fall of more than 13%. — [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/gold/news/537895)
- CNBC (31 Mar, intraday): "on track for worst month since 2008", down more than 10%. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/31/gold-on-track-for-worst-month-since-2008-as-iran-war-drags-on.html)
- IndexBox and WisdomTree also use "since 2013" framing. — [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/gold-prices-see-sharpest-monthly-drop-in-over-a-decade/); [WisdomTree](https://www.wisdomtree.com/us/insights/blog/the-month-gold-broke-five-lessons-from-the-march-madness-selloff-and-the-rebound-opportunity)

### Inferences
- Explanation of the discrepancy *(inference, not sourced data)*: (1) **Price basis.** WGC uses the LBMA PM benchmark. In June 2013 the 28 Jun PM fix was set near that day's low, so June 2013's LBMA-based fall is larger than its spot-based fall. A -12% month on LBMA therefore ranks "worst since June 2013". On spot, a -13% month exceeds June 2013's spot fall, so the comparison reaches back to October 2008. (2) **Timing.** The "2008" headlines were written intramonth on 31 Mar, at about $4,570. The month-end benchmark was $4,608, so the final loss was smaller.
- Script line: "March was gold's worst month in nearly 13 years, about -12% (World Gold Council). Some live reports that day said worst since 2008."

### Gaps
- The exact LBMA PM prints for 27 Feb and 31 Mar 2026, and the official spot month-end close, were not retrieved, so the 13% vs 12% gap is not reproduced from primary data. **The LBMA leg of the explanation is inference.**

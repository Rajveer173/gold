## 1. Gold history in US$: a verified correction table

### Takeaway
Six US$ gold episodes can be cited with reasonable confidence: 1980–99 (about −70%, a 19-year fall), May–Jun 2006 (about −22% to −26%), Mar–Oct 2008 (about −30%), 2011–15 (about −45%, high not regained until 2020), the April 2013 crash (−9% in one day, about −13% in two), and 2020–22 (about −22% intraday). The 2026 fall of about −37% is bigger than every mid-cycle correction here and smaller only than the two cycle-top bears (1980 and 2011).

**1980 → 1999**
- Gold peaked at **$850/oz on 21 Jan 1980**, driven by two oil shocks, CPI inflation of up to 14.8%, the Iranian hostage crisis and the Soviet invasion of Afghanistan — [USAGOLD](https://www.usagold.com/gold-price-history-50-years-investor-lessons/); [GoldSilver.com](https://goldsilver.com/article/gold-price-history-from-35-to-4500-in-100-years/). One source lists a $665 "1980 high", which is probably an average or a close; it conflicts with $850 **[conflict, minor]**.
- The 1999 trough is given as "almost $252", "near $250" and "$253 (1999)", a loss of about **70%** over about **19 years** — [Royal Mint](https://royalmint.com/gold-price/past-performance-of-gold); [Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/investing/gold-price-history); [Metalcharts](https://metalcharts.org/gold-price-history); [WGC, "What's a bear case for gold"](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/whats-bear-case-gold-historical-perspective/19836). Prices were still about $270 in 2001.
- **The year gold regained $850:** no retrieved source states the date. Gold first topped $1,000 in March 2008 — [CNN Money, Mar 2008](https://money.cnn.com/2008/03/13/markets/gold/index.htm). So $850 was regained in late 2007 or early 2008; the exact date is unverified (see Gaps).
- In inflation-adjusted terms the 1980 peak is about $3,300–3,600 in today's money — same search summary as above ([LBMA Alchemist 118](https://www.lbma.org.uk/alchemist/issue-118/echoes-of-the-80s-gold-prices-and-inflation-then-and-now) among the results). ## 2. Silver history in US$

### Takeaway
Silver's 1980 peak was about $49.45 (range $48.70–50.36), and Silver Thursday (27 Mar 1980) took it down to $10.80. The one-day size depends on the opening price used: −50% from $21.62, or −33% from $16.25. The 2011 peak of about $49.5–49.8 was not exceeded until **9 Oct 2025**, when silver traded above $50 for the first time ever.

## 3. Gold and silver in rupees through these episodes

### Takeaway
The rupee softened, and sometimes erased, the US$ crashes for Indian buyers. In 2013 gold fell about 28% in dollars but only about 3–5% in rupees. Domestic gold set a record of **₹34,500/10g on 28 Aug 2013**, two years *after* the dollar peak, because the rupee crashed to about 68.8/$. The flat period in rupees ran about 2012–2019: ₹31,050 → ₹35,220 annual averages, about 1.8% a year. The 2013 rupee record was surpassed only in **2019** (about 6 years), against about 9 years for the dollar high (2011 → 2020). In 2020–22 the MCX record of **₹56,191 (Aug 2020)** was regained on **13 Jan 2023** (about 2.4 years). Silver's rupee record was **₹75,020/kg on 25 Apr 2011** (not ₹73,600).

### Cited Findings
- **2013:** gold fell about 28% in USD but only about 3% in INR. It was gold's first negative year in INR in 13 years — [Business Standard, 25 Dec 2013](https://www.business-standard.com/article/markets/gold-prices-finally-fell-in-2013-113122500530_1.html) (prior notes). Separately, gold returned **−4.7% in Samvat 2069** (about Nov 2012–Nov 2013), "negative returns for the first time in fifteen Samvat years" — [Business Standard, Nov 2013](https://www.business-standard.com/article/markets/gold-offers-negative-returns-113110200569_1.html).
- **Aug 2013 rupee record:** gold hit an all-time high of **₹34,500/10g on Wed 28 Aug 2013** with the rupee at a record low of **68.75–68.85/$**. The next day it fell ₹1,575 to ₹32,325. The previous record was ₹32,975 on 27 Nov 2012 (or ₹32,500 on 26 Nov 2012 **[conflict]**). Import duty had been raised to 10% on 14 Aug 2013 — [Business Standard/PTI](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/pti-stories/gold-jumps-to-all-time-high-of-rs-34-500-on-weak-rupee-113082800348_1.html); [domain-b](https://www.domain-b.com/economy/economy-general/gold-zooms-to-a-record-rs34-500-per-10-gm); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/markets/record-rupee-plunge-pushes-gold-to-new-high-113082700966_1.html). ### Inferences
- **Recovery times, INR vs USD (gold):** the 2011–15 bear took about **9 years** in USD (Sep 2011 → 2020) but only about **6 years** in INR (Aug 2013 → 2019). In INR terms, the peak came two years later and higher, because the rupee fell from about 45 to about 68 per dollar. For a viewer who bought at the 2011 dollar top (about ₹28,000–29,000/10g, not re-verified this round), the rupee loss was much smaller still. In 2020–22 INR recovery took about **2.4 years** (Aug 2020 → Jan 2023), against a USD recovery of roughly 3.3–3.5 years (date unverified).
- **On-screen line:** "Indian buyers have historically lost less than the dollar chart shows, but have still had to wait. Gold was flat in rupees for about 7 years (2012–19)." In 2026 the duty hike to 15% played the buffer role that the rupee played in 2013. A duty cut would reverse it.
- Silver in rupees fell from ₹75,020 (2011) to about ₹30,000-something (mid-2010s), roughly −55% to −60% *(rough; the low is not verified)*. Silver regained its 2011 rupee level only around 2020 (a contested "record") or later.
